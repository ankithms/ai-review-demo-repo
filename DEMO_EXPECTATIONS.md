# Demo expectations

AI output is nondeterministic. The categories, severities, wording, matching, and finding counts below are demonstration targets, not guaranteed results. Demo branches intentionally omit only the regression coverage that would expose their planted issue; normal behavior remains covered. Never deploy them.
